About

I test defences the way an attacker would, then help build the ones that hold.

My offensive work covers black-box penetration testing, payload development, and malware engineering for authorised engagements. On the defensive side I work on application security, software supply chain integrity, and MITRE-aligned detection and mitigation for both enterprise IT and operational technology.
